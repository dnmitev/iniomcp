Privacy is built in from the start. Personal data is automatically redacted before it reaches any AI provider. We strip out IBAN numbers, social security numbers, emails, phone numbers, and addresses. Organizations can turn off redaction for internal use if they need the full data. We also redact the direct invoice links that let someone open an invoice without logging in, preventing bearer token leaks. This meets General Data Protection Regulation requirements for third-party language model providers, and it's the default behavior. You don't have to configure anything to get this protection.
