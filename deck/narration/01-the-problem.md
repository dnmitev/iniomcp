Inio clients wanted to work with their customer data, invoices, and payment information directly inside AI assistants like Claude and Cursor, but there was no way to do it. Finance teams couldn't ask an AI about payment histories. Operations staff couldn't query customer pauses. Developers couldn't check invoice retry attempts. Every single query meant leaving the assistant, opening the Inio interface, and manually finding the information. Teams had to context switch constantly between their AI tools and the Inio system. Without an integration, the data was locked out of the modern AI workspace where people increasingly do their work.
