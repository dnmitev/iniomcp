Privacy protection is automatic. Personal data gets redacted before it reaches any AI provider. We strip out IBAN numbers, social security numbers, emails, phone numbers, and addresses. We also redact the direct invoice links that let someone open an invoice without logging in, preventing bearer token leaks. Organizations can turn off redaction for internal use if they need the full data, with one environment flag. This meets General Data Protection Regulation requirements from day one. You don't configure anything. The protection is just there.
